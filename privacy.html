Privacy Policy

This app is used only by its owner to upload and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channel.

It accesses YouTube data solely through Google OAuth with the owner's consent. No user data is collected, sold, or shared with third parties. Tokens are stored locally on the owner's server and can be revoked at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
